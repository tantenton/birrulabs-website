## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a typical AI project take?
It depends on complexity. MVPs can be delivered in 2-4 weeks. Full-scale solutions typically take 3-6 months.

### Do you work with our internal team?
Yes. We typically act as an extension of your team—seamless integration, open communication.

### What makes BirruLabs different?
We focus on practical outcomes, not technology for technology's sake. If AI doesn't make your business better, we don't do it.

### Can you build from scratch?
If you don't have the required data or infrastructure, we help set up from scratch—data collection, infrastructure, and AI implementation.

### How large is your team?
We're a small team—15+ full-time experts. For large projects, we recruit specialized freelancers as needed.

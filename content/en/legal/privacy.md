# Privacy Policy BirruLabs

Published: August 4, 2026

BirruLabs respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you provide. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

## Data We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly
- Name and contact details (email, phone)
- Company/organization information
- Project data and business needs you share
- Email and chat communications

### Automatically Collected Technical Data
- IP address and browser information
- Cookies and tracking technologies
- Website usage logs

### Data from Third-Party Integrations
We do not sell your data. We may receive data from trusted partners only if you provide explicit consent.

## How We Use Your Data

- Providing and maintaining our services
- Processing your requests and sending relevant information
- Improving and developing products
- Communicating service changes
- Security and fraud prevention

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data, including:

- Data encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Data encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Limited access and multi-factor authentication
- Regular security audits
- Strict privacy policies for all employees and contractors

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data in the following cases:

- With your explicit consent
- With our service providers (vendors) who follow our privacy policy
- If required by law (court orders, etc.)
- In business transactions (acquisitions, mergers)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access personal data we store
- Correct inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (within legal limits)
- Restrict data processing
- Object to processing
- Request data portability

To exercise your rights, contact privacy@birrulabs.com.

## Cookie Policy

We use cookies to:

- Enable basic website functionality
- Understand how you use the website (analytics)
- Store your preferences
- Selected advertising and marketing

You can set your browser to reject cookies, but some features may not function properly.

## Policy Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the website or email.

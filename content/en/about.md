# About BirruLabs

We are engineers, designers, and AI researchers who believe technology should serve humanity—not the other way around.

## History

BirruLabs was founded in 2023 in Jakarta with a simple mission: to build AI that actually works for businesses and society. We saw many AI projects trapped in laboratories—technically sophisticated, yet failing in the real world.

We chose a different path. From the beginning, we built with principles: **practical over theoretical, human-centered over machine-optimized, experimental over rigid**.

## Philosophy

### Human-Centered
Technology should never drown out human needs. We start from human problems, not from available technology. Every solution we build is designed with empathy, understanding users' physical, social, and emotional contexts.

### Practical
We are not interested in "proof of concept" satisfaction. We build for production—systems that withstand real-world chaos: unstable internet connections, non-technical users, regulatory changes.

### Experimental
AI moves fast. Our strategy adapts quickly: small steps, rapid iteration, testing ideas quickly, discarding what doesn't work. We measure success based on tangible impact, not just technical metrics.

## Team

We are a small team that chooses quality over quantity. Each member brings deep expertise—from leading AI research to engineers with experience building products used by millions.

We do not work in isolation. Collaboration is central to how we operate— with you, with the open-source community, with university researchers—Together, we create something greater than the sum of its parts.

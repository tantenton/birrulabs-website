Operational platform for clinics and healthcare facilities. Combines digital medical records, doctor scheduling, patient management, and lab integration into one unified system.

Affiliate hub connecting brands with verified publisher networks. Rigorous curation, conversion optimization, and full support from our team.

AI assistant for managing social media communities. Creates content, answers questions, analyzes sentiment, and maintains consistent brand voice 24/7.

AI-powered content production platform for visuals and audio. Generates illustrations, automated video editing, responsive email headers, and other digital assets.

Digital identity verification system that streamlines user onboarding. Integration with e-KTP, face matching, and OCR with bank-grade security.

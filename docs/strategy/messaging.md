## Outlook &懊式

### What to Say
- Systems that run autonomously, amplifying your team
- Measures outcomes, not just activity
- Human-centered AI, always
- Production-First, not prototype promises

### What to Avoid
- "Autonomous agents" without context
- "AI-native" as a buzzword
- "Disrupt," "leverage," "synergy"
- Technical jargon without explanation
- "Rocket ship" growth metaphors
- Fake metrics: "1000+ users," "500+ API calls"
- Overpromising: "This will double your revenue"

## Language Style Guide

### Voice & Tone
- **Active voice:** "We build systems" not "Systems are built"
- **Direct language:** "Work while you sleep" not "Automated operation occurs"
- **Evidence-first:** Metrics before claims
- **Grounded, not hype:** Production real, not "next-gen"

### Dos and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use "production-ready" | Use "prototype" without qualification |
| Say "measurable lift" | Say "analytics magic" |
| Say "reduce manual work" | Say "replace human staff" |
| Say "real metrics" | Say "case study metrics" |
| Say "amplify your team" | Say "automate your people" |
| Say "continuous learning" | Say "AI thinks for itself" |

### Word List

**Preferred:** production, measurable, autonomous, amplification, continuous, proven, runtime, consistent, verified, human-centered

**Avoid:** next-gen, paradigm shift, disruptive, leverage, synergy, rocket ship, moonshot, AI-powered (overused), smart (meaningless), futuristic (fake), magic (unearned)

## Message Validation Checklist

Before publishing any message, verify:

- [ ] Does it claim a measurable outcome?
- [ ] Does it avoid fake metrics or vanity numbers?
- [ ] Does it center human benefit, not just AI capability?
- [ ] Does it use active voice and direct language?
- [ ] Does it avoid overpromising?
- [ ] Is it accurate to what we've built and shipped?
- [ ] Does it resonate with the specific audience?
